Rates
Note:
  • Highest Rate Applies
    If more than one statistical services is chosen, only the highest rate will be paid (e.g. if Descriptive (2 page) PLUS Inferential (1 page), with a combined page of 3, the rate is 4,900 only, which is based on inferential analysis rates, and NOT 4,000 + 1,500 = 5,500)
  • Additional Request Mid- or Post-Analysis
    If client wants to add a statistical service mid- or post-analysis (using the same dataset), and it resulted to an additional page, the rate will be based on the total number of pages the statistician provided to the client at the end. However, if no additional pages are generated, no additional payment.
  • Milestone Payment System
    An initial payment will be collected from the client. The initial payment will be equivalent to 50% of the max 2 pages. Afterwards, the statistician will ask for an online meeting to present their results on screen. Once verified by the client, the client will need to pay for the remaining payment before the statistician will forward their results in PDF and DOCX format.
